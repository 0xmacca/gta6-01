Costa Verde — an open-world crime story in the browser. Leo Marlow is out of county with no car, no cash, and a debt to the Harbor Crew. Steal cars, outrun the cops, run five jobs across the city — and walk away with the big score.

A complete 3D city on foot and by car: 7×7 blocks of streets, traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal, a 0–5 star wanted level with police cars and foot cops, a five-mission story with payouts, pistol and punch combat, a HUD with money, health, wanted stars and a live minimap, title/pause/busted/wasted screens, and a local save that keeps your money and progress. Synthesized engine, siren, gunshot and music audio. Wholly original — no names, art or music from any commercial game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network: the game is static files under game/ (three.js r187 vendored).

## Controls

WASD move / drive · Shift sprint · Space jump · Mouse drag camera · E steal car / interact · Click shoot · F punch · Enter start job · P pause · M mute
